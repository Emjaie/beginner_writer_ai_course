Module 1 of 8 · Introduction

What AI Actually Is — And How It Can Help You

Let us clear away the myths. AI is not magic, not a robot, and not complicated. It is a team of powerful assistants, waiting to help you tell your story.

Most people imagine AI as something from a science fiction film. This module exists to replace that picture with a much more useful one — and to give you the confidence to start.

👨🏾‍🏫
John's Story — Where We Begin

When John first heard about AI, he pictured a robot. He was nervous. "What if it writes better than me?" By the end of this module, John understood: AI does not replace him. It works for him.

The Honest Truth About AI

Artificial intelligence is software trained on enormous amounts of text — billions of books, articles, and websites. When you ask it a question or give it a task, it draws on all that reading to help you. That is all it is. A very well-read assistant. It cannot think for itself. It cannot feel. And it very much needs your ideas, your memories, and your voice.

❌ The Myth

"AI will write my book for me."
AI produces average-sounding text without your real stories. Only you can provide your memories and emotions.

✅ The Reality

AI helps you write your book.
It organises your ideas, improves your sentences, and suggests structure — but the heart is always yours.

❌ The Myth

"You need to be technical to use AI."
This fear keeps people away from tools that could genuinely transform their writing life.

✅ The Reality

You just need to type — or speak.
These tools work like a conversation. You write naturally and the AI responds helpfully.

The Key Skill: Giving Good Instructions

The instructions you give AI are called prompts. Three habits make a big difference:

💡 Be specific about who you are
📋 Example prompt
"I am a 67-year-old retired teacher from Nairobi writing a memoir about my years in the classroom."
💡 Tell it exactly what you want
📋 Example prompt
"Give me 10 chapter title ideas for a memoir about resilience and family in rural Kenya during the 1970s."
💡 Ask it to match your tone
📋 Example prompt
"Rewrite this paragraph to sound warm and conversational — like I am speaking to a dear friend."
📝 Exercise 1 — The Apprentice Question

Write down five things you wish you had help with in your writing. For example: "I wish someone could help me remember the right order of events." Then bring your list to your AI Tutor — Amara will tell you which tool can help and give you the exact prompt to use.

Module 2 of 8 · Voice Tools

Speak Your Book Into Existence

The most powerful writing tool for many authors over 60 is not the keyboard. It is your voice. Speak your stories and watch them become written chapters.

You do not need to type well to write a book. Winston Churchill dictated his books. Agatha Christie used a voice recorder. Today, AI makes this faster and more accessible than ever.

👨🏾‍🏫
John's Moment

John's fingers ached after ten minutes of typing. Then he discovered he could press a button, speak for twenty minutes, and watch it become a full chapter draft. "It felt like magic," he said. "But it was just a tool."

The Voice Workflow
🎙️ You Speak→ 📝 Whisper Transcribes→ 🤖 ChatGPT Drafts→ ✨ Claude Edits→ 📚 Your Chapter
Tool 1: Whisper — Speak, Get Text

Whisper by OpenAI converts your spoken audio into written text with remarkable accuracy. Access it via openai.com or through apps like Otter.ai.

  • 1

    Find a quiet place and open a voice recording app on your phone.

  • 2

    Press record and talk naturally — tell your story as if speaking to a grandchild.

  • 3

    Upload the audio to a Whisper-powered tool. It converts speech to text in seconds.

  • 4

    Copy the text and paste it into ChatGPT to create a polished chapter.

📋 Paste into ChatGPT after transcription
"Here is a transcript of me telling a story from my life. Please turn this into a warm, well-written memoir chapter. Keep my voice and personality. Add transitions and paragraph breaks where needed. Do not change the meaning or add facts I did not mention: [paste your transcript here]"
Tool 2: ChatGPT Voice Mode — Be Interviewed

On the ChatGPT app, tap the microphone to enter voice mode. ChatGPT speaks back to you — and can act as an interviewer, asking questions that draw out your memories.

📋 Say this to start your memoir interview
"I want you to interview me about my life, one question at a time. I am writing a memoir. Please ask me thoughtful questions about my childhood, my career, the challenges I have faced, and the things I am most proud of. Start with the first question now."
Tool 3: Microsoft Word Dictate

If you have Microsoft Word, you already have this feature — completely free. Click Home → Dictate (the microphone icon). Say "comma", "full stop", "new paragraph" to add punctuation as you speak.

Tool 4: Otter.ai — Record Interviews

Perfect for recording conversations with family members. Visit otter.ai — it records and transcribes conversations between multiple people.

📋 After uploading your Otter transcript to ChatGPT
"Here is a transcript of an interview with my [mother/father/relative]. Please identify the most interesting stories and write them as vivid narrative paragraphs for a family history book. Keep their authentic voice: [paste transcript]"
Tool 5: NotebookLM — Organise Everything

Upload all your transcripts to notebooklm.google.com and ask questions like:

📋 Questions to ask NotebookLM
"What themes appear most often in my interviews?" · "What stories about my childhood are in these notes?" · "Summarise everything I said about my first job."
🎙️ Exercise 2 — Speak a Memory
Tell the Story of Your First Day at School

Record yourself for at least 3 minutes — do not write anything, only speak. Transcribe using Otter.ai or Word Dictate, then paste into ChatGPT with the prompt above. Bring the result to your AI Tutor for feedback!

Module 3 of 8 · Writing & Drafting

ChatGPT — Your Writing Companion

ChatGPT is a brilliant, patient writing partner who is always available. Learn to brainstorm, outline, draft, and even get interviewed about your life.

ChatGPT is where most authors begin. Available free at chatgpt.com. Think of it as a conversation — write naturally and it responds helpfully. The more context you give, the better it works.

👨🏾‍🏫
John Uses ChatGPT

John typed: "I am a 67-year-old retired teacher from Nairobi. Give me 20 chapter ideas about growing up in Kenya in the 1960s." ChatGPT gave him 20 ideas in 20 seconds. Three of them made him cry — they were exactly the stories he had always wanted to tell.

What ChatGPT Can Do For You
💡 Brainstorm Chapter Ideas
📋 Copy this prompt
"I am writing a memoir about growing up in [your country] in the [decade]. Give me 20 chapter ideas that would make a reader feel like they were there."
📋 Create a Full Outline
📋 Copy this prompt
"Create a 12-chapter outline for a memoir about [your topic]. Each chapter should have a title and a 2-sentence summary of what happens."
✍️ Draft a Chapter
📋 Copy this prompt
"Here are my rough notes about [topic]. Please write this as a warm, engaging memoir chapter of about 600 words in first person. Keep it vivid and personal: [paste your notes]"
🎤 Ask ChatGPT to Interview You
📋 Copy this prompt
"I want you to interview me for my memoir. Ask me one question at a time about my childhood, career, and life lessons. Wait for my answer before asking the next question. Start now."
✏️ Rewrite for Tone
📋 Copy this prompt
"Rewrite this paragraph to sound warmer and more conversational — like a wise elder telling a story to their grandchildren. Do not change the meaning: [paste paragraph]"
📖 Write Your Author Biography
📋 Copy this prompt
"Write a warm and engaging author biography for the back of my memoir. Here are the key facts about me: [list your background, career, family, location]. Make it 120 words."

Claude handles long pieces of writing exceptionally well. It reads carefully and focuses on tone, flow, and emotional truth — not just grammar. Visit claude.ai for a free account.

Module 5 of 8 · Research

Perplexity AI — Your Fact Checker

Every memoir needs accurate facts. Perplexity AI finds real answers with real sources — so you can verify everything you write.

Unlike general AI tools, Perplexity searches the web in real time and provides citations for every claim. You can click the source and read the original article. Visit perplexity.ai — free with no account needed.

👨🏾‍🏫
John Checks His Facts

John searched: "What were the major droughts in Kenya in the 1960s?" Perplexity gave him exact dates, location details, and links to historical records. Now his memoir has accurate details that bring the period to life.

What to Research for Your Memoir
🗺️ Historical Events & Daily Life
📋 Search ideas for Perplexity
"What was daily life like in [your city] in [decade]?" · "What major events happened in [your country] in [year]?" · "What did school / food / transport look like in [place] in [era]?"
🌳 Family History Research
📋 Search ideas for Perplexity
"Where can I find birth and marriage records for [your country / region]?" · "What genealogy databases cover [Africa / Asia / Caribbean]?" · "How do I trace my family history using [specific records]?"
🎓 Academic Mode — For Deep Research

Perplexity has an Academic mode that searches only peer-reviewed papers. Click the "Focus" dropdown and select "Academic". Useful for health, agriculture, social history, or migration topics.

📍 Add Real Detail to Your Scenes
📋 Then paste this into ChatGPT
"I found these historical facts about [place and time]: [paste your Perplexity results]. I am writing a memoir chapter set in this time and place. Please weave these details naturally into my scene to make it feel authentic: [paste your chapter draft]"
🔍 Exercise 5 — Research Your Setting
Find Three Historical Details

Think of a chapter set in a specific place and time. Search Perplexity for three real historical details — a local event, something about daily life, or a cultural tradition. Then ask your AI Tutor: "How do I weave these facts naturally into my memoir chapter?"

Module 6 of 8 · Visuals

Napkin AI — Turn Ideas into Visuals

Napkin AI converts your written text into beautiful diagrams, timelines, and flowcharts instantly — no design skills needed.

Many authors struggle to explain ideas visually. Napkin AI solves this. Visit napkin.ai, paste text, and it offers you several beautiful visual formats instantly.

👨🏾‍🏫
John Creates a Life Timeline

John typed: "My life journey: born in Murang'a 1957, started school 1963, moved to Nairobi 1970, began teaching 1979, married 1982, retired 2018." Napkin AI turned this into a beautiful illustrated timeline in seconds — and John used it as the opening visual page of his memoir.

Four Visuals Every Memoir Needs
📅 Life Timeline
📋 Paste this kind of text into Napkin AI
"My life: Born in [place] in [year]. Started school in [year]. Moved to [place] in [year]. Started career as [job] in [year]. Got married in [year]. Had children in [years]. Retired in [year]. Began writing in [year]."
🌳 Family Tree
📋 Paste this kind of text into Napkin AI
"Family structure: Grandfather [name] married [name]. They had [number] children: [names]. My father [name] married my mother [name]. They had [number] children. I am [your name], the [first/second/third] child."
📚 Chapter Theme Map
📋 Paste this kind of text into Napkin AI
"My memoir has these main themes that connect: Childhood and roots. Education and becoming a teacher. Marriage and family. Struggles and resilience. Legacy and lessons learned. Each theme grows from the one before."
🗺️ Journey Map
📋 Paste this kind of text into Napkin AI
"My journey: Started in [village/town]. Moved to [city] for school. Worked in [place] for [years]. Then moved to [place]. Eventually settled in [place]. The journey taught me [lesson]."
🎨 Exercise 6 — Create Your Life Timeline
Your Story, Visualised

Module 7 of 8 · Design

AI Images — Book Covers & Illustrations

A beautiful cover sells your memoir before anyone reads a single page. AI image generation makes professional-quality illustrations possible — without hiring a designer.

You do not need design skills. You only need words. Describe what you want in plain language and AI creates it. The better your description, the more beautiful the result.

👨🏾‍🏫
John Designs His Cover

John typed: "A watercolour illustration of a Kenyan village schoolroom in the 1970s, warm afternoon light through the windows, children at wooden desks, soft earth tones." The image made him emotional. It was exactly what he had pictured for forty years.

Tool 1: ChatGPT Image Generation

ChatGPT (paid plan) generates images directly in chat. Simply describe what you want in detail.

📋 Book cover image prompt
"Create a watercolour book cover for a memoir titled '[Your Title]'. The image should show [describe your scene — people, place, era, mood]. Use warm, earthy colours. Leave space at the top for the title in large text."
📋 Chapter illustration prompt
"A detailed illustration of [scene from your book — e.g., a rural African market in the 1960s, sellers with colourful cloth and vegetables, busy and full of life]. Style: soft watercolour painting. Warm, nostalgic mood."
Tool 2: Canva AI — Free and Easy

Visit canva.com (free account). Click Create a Design → Book Cover. Use the AI image generator built inside Canva — it is very simple to use.

📋 Social media book announcement
"Create a book announcement social media post for a memoir titled '[Your Title]' by [Your Name]. Background: [colour]. Feature the book cover. Include the text: 'Coming soon'. Make it elegant and memorable."
Tips for Better Image Prompts
  • 1

    Describe the mood: "warm and nostalgic", "hopeful", "bittersweet", "celebratory"

  • 2

    Name a style: "watercolour painting", "oil painting", "pencil sketch", "vintage photograph style"

  • 3

    Include era and location: "1970s rural Kenya", "1950s Nairobi city centre", "1960s Caribbean village"

  • 4

    Describe the light: "warm afternoon sunlight", "golden hour", "soft morning mist"

Upload transcripts to NotebookLM to find themes. Use ChatGPT to build your chapter outline.

Step 4 · Draft Chapters — ChatGPT

Paste your transcript and notes into ChatGPT. Ask it to draft each chapter. Work through chapter by chapter.

Step 5 · Research & Verify — Perplexity AI

Fact-check dates, events, and historical details. Add real historical colour and accuracy to your memoir.

Step 6 · Edit & Refine — Claude

Paste each chapter into Claude for professional editing. Preserve your voice, strengthen your scenes.

Step 7 · Create Visuals — Napkin AI / Canva

Build your timeline, family tree, and chapter maps with Napkin. Design your cover and illustrations with Canva.

Step 8 · Share Your Book — Print / eBook / Social Media

Export to Word and send to a local printer, publish as a PDF, or share digitally. Host a book launch!
